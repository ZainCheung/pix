---
title: Pix Documentation
description: Use Pi from your iPhone while Pi, your code, and your sessions stay on your computer.
---

Pix lets your iPhone control Pi while Pi runs on your Mac or Linux computer.
The phone connects to that host; Pix does not move your working environment into
a hosted service.
